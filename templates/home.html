MEMORY AI GAME

Challenge the AI in this retro 8-bit battle!

RULES

  • Numbers are revealed for 5 seconds at game start
  • Click tiles to reveal numbers (1-9)
  • You gain points for each tile you pick
  • Higher numbers = more points
  • The AI takes turns picking tiles
  • Game ends when all tiles are revealed
  • Highest score wins!
